# Commercial Positioning

## What this product is

An editorial operating system for macroeconomics YouTube teams:

- upstream topic discovery from authoritative news sources
- topic prioritization by virality, timing, competition, and rough economics
- human-in-the-loop script pipeline with approvals and version history

It should not be sold as a generic "AI script writer".
It should be sold as a workflow layer for research-heavy channels that need better hit-rate and more control.

## Best initial customer

- small YouTube editorial teams
- independent macro / geopolitics channels
- producer-led channels with one host and one editor
- newsletter + YouTube hybrids that already research topics manually

## Why it can win

Most adjacent tools do one of these things:

- SEO and YouTube optimization only
- trend spotting only
- generic AI writing only
- broad media intelligence for enterprises

This product combines:

1. macro topic discovery
2. editorial angle formation
3. evidence-backed script workflow
4. human approval checkpoints

That makes it much more specific and operational for a serious economics channel.

## Suggested pricing

### Starter

- $79-$149 / month
- 1 workspace
- limited topic radar refresh
- limited AI generations
- no team collaboration

### Pro Team

- $299-$499 / month
- team workspace
- full pipeline
- approvals and versioning
- topic radar and competitor signals
- export and template library

### Studio / Agency

- $900+ / month or custom
- multiple channels
- multiple workspaces
- priority support
- custom source packs
- white-glove setup

## Pricing logic

This should price above generic creator SEO tools and below enterprise media intelligence platforms.

The commercial story is:

- cheaper than wasting one weak video idea per month
- cheaper than manual research + prompt-chaos + rework
- more controllable than one-shot AI script generation

## What must be true before serious selling

- AI fallback and error handling are stable
- topic radar stays curated and deduplicated
- virality scoring stays explainable
- pipeline artifacts are clearly versioned
- producer can move from candidate topic to final script without losing context
